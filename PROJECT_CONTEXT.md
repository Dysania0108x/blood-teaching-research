# Blood Teaching Research — Project Context

## Project purpose
Develop a journal manuscript based on the teacher's existing lesson materials for the junior-high biology topic “Blood”. The target journal style and structure are to be learned from the supplied *生物学教学* journal articles. Do not confuse this manuscript with the separate Grassland Ecosystem project.

## Source materials and evidence boundaries
The teacher identified three lesson-related source files as the basis for the Blood manuscript:
1. “血液”教学设计 (lesson plan)
2. The corresponding teaching presentation / slides
3. The group activity / worksheet materials

A separate *生物学教学* journal issue/article is a writing-method and formatting reference, not a source of facts about the Blood lesson itself.

The currently available lesson-plan extract establishes:
- Topic: “血液”, Section 1 of Chapter 15, “人体内物质的运输”, Grade 8, first semester.
- Prior learning: nutrition and respiration; Blood provides a basis for understanding transport of nutrients and gases and prepares for later learning about waste excretion and hormone transport.
- Prior student knowledge: students have everyday experience of bleeding and blood tests, but may not understand blood components, their functions, or links to life activities.
- Lesson objectives include structural-functional understanding; interpreting routine blood-test reports; scientific inquiry, reasoning and collaboration; valuing life and social responsibility.
- Key point: describe blood components and their main functions.
- Difficulty: use learned knowledge to explain and solve everyday problems related to blood.
- Materials: blood separation demonstration, human blood smear, microscope.
- Lesson sequence in the plan: contextual introduction from bleeding experience; observe fish-tail blood flow; distinguish coagulated blood/serum and discuss anticoagulant; observe anticoagulated blood separation into plasma and blood cells; use microscope to observe blood smear and identify cells; group inquiry (plasma composition/function; red blood cell structure-function; blood count report reasoning; model blood clotting); groups present; consolidate component-function framework; interpret teacher's own blood-test report; discuss blood volume and voluntary donation, then group outreach task.

Important: The three user-identified files must be examined together before deciding the final paper's central innovation. This README does not yet claim to have incorporated all slide and worksheet content.

## Required workflow
1. Read the lesson plan, presentation, and group activity sheets fully.
2. Inspect the supplied *生物学教学* example article for structure, headings, abstract, keywords, figures/tables, teaching-process presentation and reflection style.
3. Compare source materials and identify the strongest evidence-supported teaching features.
4. Propose a paper thesis, title options and section outline before drafting.
5. Draft only from the lesson source materials; clearly label any proposed additions.
6. Verify all citations and bibliographic details. Never invent classroom outcomes or student data.

## Separation rule
Grassland Ecosystem and Blood are separate manuscripts with separate lesson materials, teaching goals, evidence and GitHub tasks.
